# Private Car Package Policy: Policy Wording (Demo)

Issued by: Redstring Demo General Insurance (fictional insurer used for demonstration only).
Product code: PCP-OD-2026

## 1. Definitions
1.1 **Insured Declared Value (IDV)** is the maximum amount payable for total loss or theft of the vehicle in the policy period, as stated in the Schedule.
1.2 **Policy Period** is the period stated in the Schedule. Cover begins at 00:00 on the start date and ends at 23:59 on the end date.
1.3 **Network Garage** is a garage empanelled by the Company for cashless repairs.
1.4 **Insured Vehicle** is the vehicle whose registration number is stated in the Schedule.

## 2. Own Damage Cover
2.1 The Company will indemnify the Insured against loss of or damage to the Insured Vehicle caused by: (a) accidental external means; (b) fire, explosion, self-ignition or lightning; (c) burglary, housebreaking or theft; (d) riot, strike or malicious act; (e) flood, storm, cyclone, inundation or earthquake; (f) transit by road, rail or inland waterway.
2.2 The maximum liability for any one claim shall not exceed the IDV stated in the Schedule.
2.3 Repairs at a Network Garage may be settled cashless. Repairs elsewhere are settled on reimbursement against an original GST invoice.

## 3. Deductible
3.1 A compulsory deductible of INR 1,000 applies to each claim for vehicles up to 1500 cc, and INR 2,000 for vehicles above 1500 cc.
3.2 Any voluntary deductible stated in the Schedule applies in addition to the compulsory deductible.

## 4. Exclusions
The Company shall not be liable for:
4.1 Consequential loss, depreciation, wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, failure or breakage.
4.2 Damage while the vehicle is driven by a person under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
4.3 Damage while the vehicle is driven by a person who does not hold a valid driving licence.
4.4 Damage while the vehicle is used for hire or reward, racing, speed testing or outside the Geographical Area (India).
4.5 Damage that occurred before the start of the Policy Period, or damage not caused by the reported incident.
4.6 Loss or damage caused intentionally by the Insured or with the Insured's connivance.
4.7 Any claim where the incident date falls outside the Policy Period.

## 5. Depreciation on Replaced Parts
5.1 Rubber, nylon and plastic parts, tyres, tubes and batteries: 50%.
5.2 Fibreglass components: 30%.
5.3 Glass parts: nil.
5.4 Metal parts: 0% (vehicle age up to 6 months), 5% (6–12 months), 10% (1–2 years), 15% (2–3 years), 25% (3–4 years), 35% (4–5 years).
5.5 Labour charges are payable in full; painting materials are subject to 25% depreciation on the material component.

## 6. Claims Procedure
6.1 The Insured must notify the Company within 48 hours of the incident.
6.2 Every claim must include: (a) a signed claim form; (b) a copy of the Registration Certificate; (c) a copy of the driving licence of the person driving at the time; (d) a repair estimate or original GST invoice; (e) photographs of the damage.
6.3 A police report (FIR) is mandatory for theft, burglary, malicious damage, third-party injury, and any accident where the claim exceeds INR 1,00,000.
6.4 The Company may appoint a surveyor to inspect the vehicle before repairs for claims above INR 50,000.
6.5 Claims reported within 30 days of policy inception, or on policies issued after a break in insurance, are referred to the Special Investigation Unit before settlement.
6.6 The invoice must be issued in the name of the Insured for the Insured Vehicle, and dated on or after the incident date.

## 7. Fraud
7.1 If any claim is fraudulent in any respect, or if any false declaration is made or used in support of it, or if fraudulent means are used to obtain any benefit, all benefits under this policy in respect of that claim are forfeited.
7.2 The Company may share claim information with industry fraud repositories and law enforcement.

## 8. No Claim Bonus
8.1 A No Claim Bonus applies at renewal if no claim was made in the preceding policy period: 20% after one claim-free year, rising to a maximum of 50%.
