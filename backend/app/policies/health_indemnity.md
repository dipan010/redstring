# Family Health Indemnity Policy: Policy Wording (Demo)

Issued by: Redstring Demo General Insurance (fictional insurer used for demonstration only).
Product code: FHI-2026

## 1. Definitions
1.1 **Sum Insured** is the maximum amount payable for all claims in a policy year, as stated in the Schedule.
1.2 **Insured Person** means a person named in the Schedule as covered under this policy.
1.3 **Hospitalisation** means admission to a hospital for at least 24 consecutive hours, except for the day-care procedures listed in Annexure A.
1.4 **Network Hospital** means a hospital empanelled by the Company for cashless treatment.

## 2. Cover
2.1 The Company will reimburse reasonable and customary medical expenses incurred on hospitalisation of an Insured Person for illness or injury during the Policy Period, up to the Sum Insured.
2.2 Covered expenses include room charges, nursing, surgeon and anaesthetist fees, operation theatre charges, medicines and consumables used during hospitalisation, and diagnostic tests.
2.3 Pre-hospitalisation expenses for 30 days and post-hospitalisation expenses for 60 days are covered when related to the same illness.

## 3. Limits
3.1 Room rent is limited to 1% of the Sum Insured per day. If a higher room category is chosen, all associated expenses are paid proportionately.
3.2 Cataract surgery is limited to INR 40,000 per eye.
3.3 A co-payment of 10% applies to every claim for Insured Persons aged 60 years or above.

## 4. Waiting Periods
4.1 Any illness contracted within the first 30 days from the policy start date is not covered, except injury due to an accident.
4.2 The following conditions are covered only after 24 months of continuous cover: cataract, hernia, joint replacement (knee or hip), kidney stones, gall bladder stones, sinusitis, and benign prostate enlargement.
4.3 Pre-existing diseases declared at inception are covered only after 36 months of continuous cover.

## 5. Exclusions
The Company shall not be liable for:
5.1 Treatment that is not medically necessary, or admission primarily for diagnostic tests.
5.2 Cosmetic or aesthetic treatment, unless required due to an accident.
5.3 Expenses not supported by the treating doctor's prescription or discharge summary.
5.4 Treatment outside India.

## 6. Claims Procedure
6.1 The Company must be notified within 48 hours of an emergency admission, or 72 hours before a planned admission.
6.2 Reimbursement claims must include: (a) a signed claim form; (b) the original final hospital bill with itemised breakup; (c) the discharge summary; (d) prescriptions and pharmacy bills; (e) investigation reports.
6.3 The final bill must be issued in the name of the Insured Person, dated on or after the date of admission, and match the treatment in the discharge summary.

## 7. Fraud
7.1 If any claim is fraudulent in any respect, or if any false declaration is made or used in support of it, all benefits under this policy in respect of that claim are forfeited.
7.2 The Company may share claim information with industry fraud repositories and law enforcement.
