# Home Shield Policy: Policy Wording (Demo)

Issued by: Redstring Demo General Insurance (fictional insurer used for demonstration only).
Product code: HSP-2026

## 1. Definitions
1.1 **Insured Property** means the building and contents at the address stated in the Schedule.
1.2 **Sum Insured** is stated separately for the building and for contents in the Schedule.
1.3 **Policy Period** is the period stated in the Schedule.

## 2. Cover
2.1 The Company will indemnify the Insured against loss of or damage to the Insured Property caused by: (a) fire, lightning or explosion; (b) storm, cyclone, flood or inundation; (c) burst or overflowing water pipes and tanks; (d) burglary or housebreaking involving forcible and violent entry or exit; (e) riot, strike or malicious damage.
2.2 Repairs are settled on reimbursement against a contractor's invoice or the cost of replacement for contents, less depreciation.

## 3. Deductible and Depreciation
3.1 A deductible of INR 5,000 applies to each claim.
3.2 Contents are subject to depreciation of 10% per year of age, up to a maximum of 50%.

## 4. Exclusions
The Company shall not be liable for:
4.1 Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, damp, rot or seepage over time.
4.2 Theft without forcible and violent entry, or theft by a family member or domestic help.
4.3 Loss or damage when the property has been unoccupied for more than 30 consecutive days.
4.4 Loss or damage that occurred before the start of the Policy Period.
4.5 Loss at any address other than the Insured Property.

## 5. Claims Procedure
5.1 The Insured must notify the Company within 7 days of the incident.
5.2 Every claim must include: (a) a signed claim form; (b) photographs of the damage; (c) a contractor's estimate or invoice for repairs, or proof of purchase for contents.
5.3 A police report (FIR) is mandatory for burglary, theft and malicious damage. A fire brigade report is required for fire claims above INR 1,00,000.
5.4 Claims reported within 30 days of policy inception are referred to the Special Investigation Unit before settlement.
5.5 The contractor's invoice must be for work at the Insured Property and dated on or after the incident date.

## 6. Fraud
6.1 If any claim is fraudulent in any respect, or if any false declaration is made or used in support of it, all benefits under this policy in respect of that claim are forfeited.
6.2 The Company may share claim information with industry fraud repositories and law enforcement.
